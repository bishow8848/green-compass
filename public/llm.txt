# Mardi Treks — Company & Trekking Information

## Company Overview
Mardi Treks is a premier trekking and tour agency based in Pokhara, Nepal. Founded by local trekking experts, we specialize in guided trekking expeditions, cultural tours, and climbing adventures across Nepal's Himalayan regions. We have 8+ years of experience, 15+ certified guides, and have served 2,500+ trekkers from 40+ countries.

## Important Trekking Information
- **Best seasons**: Spring (Mar-May) and Autumn (Sep-Nov) for clear views and stable weather
- **Permits required**: TIMS card + ACAP/national park permit (handled by Mardi Treks)
- **Guide requirement**: Mandatory by Nepal law for foreign trekkers on most routes
- **Insurance**: Comprehensive travel insurance with helicopter evacuation coverage up to 5,000m is mandatory
- **Accommodation**: Tea houses and lodges along all major trekking routes
- **Difficulty levels**: Easy, Moderate, Challenging — suitable for beginners with good fitness
